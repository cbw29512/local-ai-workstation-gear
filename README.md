# Local AI Workstation Gear

This repository manages the workflow for researching, selecting, and publishing Amazon affiliate products for local AI workstation gear, with a focus on home organization.

## Structure

The repository contains:

1. **Product Research & Candidate Management**
   - Cloud vertical research packets defining product categories
   - Approved candidate queues for products ready for affiliate linking
   - Manual AI handoff files for research requests

2. **Data Organization**
   - Amazon affiliate links from the queue
   - Active large AI research requests
   - Product candidate clarification packets
   - Site portfolio documentation including vertical proposals

3. **Workflow Automation**
   - Handoff processes between AI research and human review
   - Safety locks preventing accidental publishing/affiliate changes
   - Approval workflows for product candidates

## Main Folders

- `/data/site_portfolio/` - Site content, verticals, and portfolio management
- `/data/product_candidates/` - Candidate products with clarification packets
- `/data/large_ai_bridge/` - AI research request and handoff management
- `/data/amazon_links/` - Amazon affiliate link management

This project supports local AI workstation gear sales through structured, human-reviewed product research.
